ABOUT US

Making Bookkeeping Simple and Accurate

CRT Financial System is a web-based accounting platform designed to simplify the process of recording and reporting the financial activities of a small service business. Our goal is to provide a faster, more organized, and easier way of following the accounting cycle, from recording transactions to preparing financial statements.

From service revenue and expenses to purchases of supplies and equipment, owner investments, and loans, users can easily record transactions, review the General Journal, check the Trial Balance, and generate the Income Statement and Balance Sheet.

Mission

To provide a reliable and user-friendly accounting system that helps students and small businesses record transactions correctly and understand how financial reports are prepared.

Vision

To become a simple and efficient bookkeeping solution that encourages accuracy, accountability, and better financial decision-making.
